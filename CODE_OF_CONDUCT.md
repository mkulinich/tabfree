# Code of Conduct

TabFree adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, chat and events.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism focused on the work, not the person. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Until the project has a public home and named maintainers, report concerns privately to the project maintainers. When the repository goes public, a dedicated contact address will be added here, and reports will be handled confidentially under the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
